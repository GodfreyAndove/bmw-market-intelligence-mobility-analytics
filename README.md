Conclusion
The dataset presents a realistic simulation of adolescent digital behavior and mental health interactions. The strongest patterns indicate that: anxiety, 
stress, and digital addiction are the most influential contributors to mental health risk.
Meanwhile, direct digital behavior measures such as total social media time show surprisingly weak relationships, suggesting the dataset prioritizes 
psychological state variables over behavioral exposure variables in determining outcomes.
Overall, it is a useful dataset for educational analytics, predictive modeling practice, and exploratory behavioral research.
